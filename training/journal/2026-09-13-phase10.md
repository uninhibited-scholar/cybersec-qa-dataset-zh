## Distillation decision

Response distillation is feasible with API teachers; logits distillation is not
available from black-box remote teachers. A staged plan was added at
`training/plans/distillation.md`. Do not mix teacher outputs into the clean
training set until provenance, evidence boundaries and safety are reviewed.
