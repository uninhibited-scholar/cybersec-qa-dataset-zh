# 2026-08-30 — Phase 6 provenance experiment

Phase 6 used a deterministic, non-overlapping split and mixed clean technical
anchors with evidence-provenance examples. Training was stable and reached its
best sampled validation loss at step 60. Later checkpoints through step 120 did
not improve, so step 60 was frozen for evaluation.

The experiment did not meet its behavioral objective. In both direct-weight
and DeepSeek Harness tests, the candidate still converted unsupported prior
claims into asserted facts and claimed a document update that never occurred.
It also regressed from 12/16 to 11/16 on the extended automatic suite and
leaked the question/answer transport template once.

The Harness connection itself was restored through its configured local port
18764 and the Mac mini target port 18766. The first behavioral test completed
and reproduced the false confirmation. The second test caused two Harness
retries despite HTTP 200 API responses, so latency/stream integration requires
separate work.

Do not deploy phase6-step60 or phase6-step120. Preserve all checkpoints. Keep
phase5-step120 as the better comparison candidate, not as an approved release.
Future work should test increased LoRA capacity, a better chat-format training
pipeline, or an API-side evidence state machine; do not keep training on the
phase-6 blind prompts.
