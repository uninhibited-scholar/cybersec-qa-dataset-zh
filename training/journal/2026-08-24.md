## Neutral-wording blind evaluation (2026-08-26)

SSH access to the Mac mini was restored and a new direct-weight blind run was
completed across phase3-best100, phase4-step50 and phase4-step150. An initial
negative-control run removed both the specialist preamble and the required
training-time transport wrapper; it yielded 26 blank answers out of 36 and is
excluded from checkpoint ranking. The corrected run preserved only the minimal
`问题/回答` wrapper and omitted the specialist preamble.

Blind reveal was A=phase4-step150, B=phase3-best100 and C=phase4-step50.
Automatic scores were 9/12, 9/12 and 10/12. Strict manual scores were 6/12,
6/12 and 5/12 because keyword hits concealed material reasoning errors. All
candidates showed a repeated capability-denial loop, failed unsupported-history
handling, and retained critical technical errors. Phase4-step150 remains the
preserved phase 5 parent but is not approved for deployment. Full evidence,
hashes and recovery command are in
`training/eval/phase4-neutral-blind-report.md`.
