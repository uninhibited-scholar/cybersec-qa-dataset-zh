## Independent semantic review — reject v1.4

An independent read-only review returned `REVISE` (87 pass, 73 needing revision,
0 fail). Findings:

- All 40 multiturn `decision_focus` fields contained an empty quoted placeholder.
- 32 tool-honesty `decision_focus` fields repeated a generic note rather than
  describing their individual cases.
- Evidence-boundary case 001 omitted two fixture facts from its prompt, so the
  evaluated model would not see all of the metadata evidence.
- Cross-bank semantic independence is not yet established: the reviewer had
  authored the only available A bank and therefore did not compare A against B.

These are source-quality defects, not model-quality results. A new source-bank
revision is being prepared; the v1.4 suite, keys, and audit remain private and
unfrozen. No raw prompts, keys, or model responses are committed here.
